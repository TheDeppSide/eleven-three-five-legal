FACET Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 1, 2026

Overview

FACET is developed by ELEVEN THREE FIVE.

This Privacy Policy explains how FACET handles information when you use the app.

Data Collection

FACET does not collect, transmit, sell, or share personal data with ELEVEN THREE FIVE or third parties.

FACET does not use user accounts, analytics, advertising, marketing tracking, crash-reporting services, remote databases, or cloud synchronization operated by ELEVEN THREE FIVE.

Data Stored on Your Device

FACET stores information locally on your device in order to provide its features. This may include:

  • app settings and preferences;
  • focus-session history, including session times, durations, and completion or interruption state;
  • your optional custom HYPE message;
  • local notification configuration and scheduling information.

This information is processed locally by FACET and is not transmitted to ELEVEN THREE FIVE.

Android Backup and Device Transfer

Depending on your Android and Google backup settings, Android may back up or transfer eligible FACET app data as part of the operating system's backup or device-transfer services.

These backups are managed by Android or the user's device or account provider. ELEVEN THREE FIVE does not operate this backup service and cannot access the contents of your Android backup.

Notifications

FACET can schedule local notifications related to focus sessions. These notifications are created and scheduled on your device and do not use a remote push-notification service.

If you create a custom HYPE message, that message may appear in a local completion notification and may therefore be visible on your lock screen depending on your device notification settings.

Payments

FACET may be purchased through Google Play. Payment processing is handled by Google Play.

FACET does not receive or store payment-card details, billing addresses, or payment credentials.

Third-Party Libraries

FACET uses software libraries for functionality such as local preferences, notifications, display-awake behavior, localization, package information, and visual asset rendering.

FACET does not include advertising, analytics, attribution, or third-party tracking SDKs.

Data Retention and Deletion

Locally stored settings and session history remain on your device until they are changed or deleted, the app's storage is cleared, or FACET is uninstalled.

Individual session-history entries can be deleted from within FACET.

Android-managed backups may remain available according to your Android or Google account backup settings even after the local app installation is removed.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated if FACET's functionality or data practices change.

The effective date at the top of this page will be updated when material changes are made.
